Topic: Resistance Measurement using Wheatstone Bridge Circuit

Objectives

  • To study the working principles of the Wheatstone Bridge circuit.
  • To practice balancing the bridge circuit by measuring the galvanometer current until it reaches zero.
  • To determine the unknown resistance (Rx) from the bridge balance condition.

Theory

A Wheatstone Bridge circuit consists of four resistors (R1, R2, R3, and Rx) connected in a diamond configuration. A DC power source (V) is connected across nodes A and C, and a galvanometer is connected between nodes B and D to measure the electric current (Ig).

Balanced Bridge Condition:

When the resistance value of R3 is adjusted until the electric potentials at node B and node D are equal (VB = VD), no current will flow through the galvanometer, which means:

Ig = 0

When the circuit is balanced, the relationship between the resistance ratios is given by the equation:

R1 / R2 = R3 / Rx

Therefore, the unknown resistance (Rx) can be calculated using the equation:

Rx = (R2 × R3) / R1

Procedure

  1. Start Experiment: Click the "Randomize Rx" button to generate a random unknown resistance value.
  2. Set R1 and R2: Adjust the resistance values for R1 and R2 on the sliders as appropriate.
  3. Balance the Circuit (R3):
    • Adjust slider R3 or use the fine-tuning buttons (+1, -1, +10, -10) until the galvanometer needle points to 0.
    • Alternatively, click the "Auto Tune Balance" button to immediately find the balance point.
  4. Calculate Rx: Use the balanced values of R1, R2, and R3 to calculate Rx using the formula.
  5. Record Data: Click the "Record Data" button to store the experimental results into the data table.
  6. Repeat Experiment: Click "Randomize Rx" or change the R1/R2 ratio, then repeat the experiment for comparison.
