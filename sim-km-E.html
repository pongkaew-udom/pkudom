Experimental Procedure for Simulating Atmospheric Optical Phenomena in Virtual Physics Lab

  1. Phenomenon Selection

    In the "🎯 1. Phenomenon Selection" section, select the optical phenomenon you wish to generate, including:

    • 🌈 1. Rainbow
    • ☀️ 2. Sun Dog
    • ☁️ 3. Cloud Iridescence
    • ⭕ 4. Solar Halo
  2. Scenario Settings
    • Particle Type Selection:
      • Large Spherical Raindrops (Raindrops > 0.5 mm)
      • Uniform Micro Droplets (Cloud Droplets 2-5 µm)
      • Horizontally Aligned Hexagonal Ice Plates (Flat Plates)
      • Randomly Oriented Hexagonal Ice Columns (Column Crystals)
    • Adjust Sun Elevation: Slide the angle adjustment bar within the range of 0 degrees to 80 degrees.
    • Select Observer Direction:
      • Look away from Sun
      • Look towards Sun
    • Adjust Particle Uniformity / Density: Slide the level bar from 1 to 10.
  3. Testing & Observation
    • Click the "🧪 Test Scenario" button to run the simulation.
    • If settings are physically correct: The system will display the phenomenon on the "🏞️ Outdoor Unified Scene" along with a green notification box explaining the cause of the phenomenon.
    • If settings are incorrect: The system will display a red notification message in the status box indicating the reasons why the phenomenon failed to occur.
