Lab Worksheet: Measuring Capacitance with a Digital Multimeter

Virtual Laboratory - Capacitance Measurement

1. Equipment

  • Digital Multimeter for measuring electrical capacitance
  • Capacitors, rating C = 100.0 nF, 10 units

2. Theory

A Capacitor is an electronic component that stores electrical charge, measured in Farads (F). When N identical capacitors, each with capacitance C, are connected together in a circuit, the total equivalent capacitance (Ctotal) is determined as follows:

  • Series Connection: Connecting capacitors end-to-end increases the effective separation between the plates, resulting in a decrease in total capacitance. Calculated using:
    1 / Ctotal = (1/C₁) + (1/C₂) + ... + (1/CN)  →  For identical capacitors: Ctotal = C / N
  • Parallel Connection: Connecting capacitors side-by-side increases the effective surface area of the plates, resulting in an increase in total capacitance. Calculated using:
    Ctotal = C₁ + C₂ + ... + CN  →  For identical capacitors: Ctotal = N × C

3. Procedure

Part 1: Measuring Total Capacitance in Series Connection

  1. Open the virtual lab simulation and observe the initial default settings.
  2. Select the connection mode as "Series" on the control panel.
  3. Set the "Number of Capacitors (N)" slider to 1 unit.
  4. Read the theoretical calculated capacitance (Ccalc) and the measured value displayed on the digital multimeter.
  5. Click the "Record to Table" button to save the measurement to the data table.
  6. Increase the number of capacitors N to 2, 4, and 10 units, respectively. Observe the changes in circuit wiring/plate visualization and record the measured values each time.

Part 2: Measuring Total Capacitance in Parallel Connection

  1. Switch the connection mode to "Parallel" on the control panel.
  2. Set the "Number of Capacitors (N)" slider to 1 unit.
  3. Read the measured value from the multimeter display and click "Record to Table".
  4. Increase the number of capacitors N to 2, 5, and 10 units, respectively. Observe the parallel circuit arrangement on screen and record the results into the data table.
